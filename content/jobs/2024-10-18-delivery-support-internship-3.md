We are looking for you!
If you want to work and grow in a dynamic company transforming the IoT industry, look no further - Zerynth is your playground! We’re seeking a motivated, talented person who is interested in the application of Lean principles in varied processes and in collaboration with diversified teams.

What you will do:
Support the team in gaining a comprehensive understanding of customer workflows and use cases.
Assist in gathering and documenting clear technical requirements from customers.
Provide technical assistance in the design and implementation of custom Industrial IoT (IIoT) solutions.
Participate in the development and installation of IIoT applications at customer sites.
Help monitor and oversee customer projects, ensuring solutions are effectively deployed and operating smoothly.

What skills we need:
A Bachelor's or Master's degree in Electronic Engineering, Automation Engineering, Computer Science, Biomedical Engineering, or a related field is preferred but not mandatory.
A technical diploma in these fields, or relevant practical experience, will also be considered.
Proficiency in programming, especially with Python, scripting, network API interfaces, and SQL.
Bonus skills: Knowledge of Git and experience with Docker.
Familiarity with technology integration, including mounting, cabling, wiring, hot-staging, and experience working with power and data signals.
General understanding of sensing applications
Fluency in English.
A proactive, self-starter attitude with strong communication skills and an innovative, out-of-the-box mindset.
Ability to work effectively within a multidisciplinary and dynamic team environment

Application process: Candidates will have a first interview that, if successful, will lead to a skill assessment phase. 

Contractual offer: Full Time - Internship (3 or 6 months).

Expense Reimbursement: The internship includes a reimbursement of expenses, ensuring that you are supported financially during your training period.

Location: Italy. Hybrid and flexible work. Presence at Zerynth's R&D offices in Pisa is required for the onboarding period and based on planned activities.

About Zerynth
Zerynth supports manufacturing companies in streamlining production by increasing the value of interconnected industrial assets. Through the Zerynth Industrial IoT & AI Platform, it connects any industrial machinery in less than 3 hours, using a plug-and-play and non-invasive approach, enabling a complete 4.0 transformation with simplicity, speed and security.
Founded in 2015, Zerynth counts a team of over 40 experienced IoT professionals, strategic partnerships with system integrators and technology providers such as SAP, as well as over 150 customers across diverse industries: discrete manufacturing, food & beverage, logistics, utilities, and machinery manufacturing. Headquartered in Pisa, Italy, Zerynth is actively pursuing an ambitious international expansion project.
