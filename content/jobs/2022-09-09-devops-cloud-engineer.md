This is an exciting opportunity to join our team in the role of DevOps, Cloud Engineer. You will develop your career in our Cloud team, contributing to define and implement a robust, scalable, pragmatic and realistic cloud solution that works for our clients, proportionally to your level of experience. You will write Infrastructure as Code (IaC) and develop application deployment automation using industry standard deployment and configuration tools. You will design and implement continuous delivery pipelines that serve the purpose of provisioning and operating client test as well as production environments, providing support to systems, including responding to monitoring alerts. You will Implement and stay abreast of Cloud and DevOps industry best practices and tooling.

You will collaborate with top-notch clients and become part of a great team, where there is room for improvement at all levels. You will have continuous education, prospects for professional development and a modern and enjoyable work environment.

Experience using one or more cloud provider such as Azure or AWS.
Experience with IAC tools (Terraform)

Experience with Containers orchestration (such as Kubenetes, Docker)

Experience with Configuration management tools (such as Chef )

Experience with Source control management tool  (Git)
