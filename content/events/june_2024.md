📢 Pisa.dev is back with an exciting summer event! 🍉 🌴 🍹

⏰ Join us on 19 June at 18.30 in Aula G of the Polo Fibonacci for our meetup followed by dinner (location to be defined)

👀 The first speaker will be Federico Paolinelli! He will share his expertise on how to deliver an engaging talk, covering everything from choosing a topic to creating impactful slides. This is a fantastic opportunity to learn valuable tips and tricks from an experienced speaker like Federico.

🚀 The second speaker will be Giovanni Toraldo! He will discuss Kubernetes explaining the core concepts.

🍕 As always, we'll wrap up the evening with pizza and beer at a local pizzeria.

Details:

Speaker: Federico Paolinelli
Title: From zero to your first talk
Abstract: This is a meta talk: a talk about how to make a talk. If you are interested in giving your first talk, I will go from finding the right topic to working on the slides, to many of the other small tips I learned the hard way.

Speaker: Giovanni Toraldo
Title: Just Enough Kubernetes
Abstract: Kubernetes has emerged as a dominant platform for managing containerized applications, yet its complexity can be daunting for those new to the technology. We will deep dive into the core concepts of Kubernetes and try running a single application into a local cluster.
