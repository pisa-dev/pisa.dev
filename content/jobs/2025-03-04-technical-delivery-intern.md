Join Zerynth and grow in a dynamic company that is transforming the IoT industry! We’re looking for a motivated and talented individual eager to develop their skills in diverse processes and collaborate with multidisciplinary teams.

What you will do:
Support the team in understanding customer workflows and use cases.
Assist in gathering and documenting technical requirements from customers.
Contribute to the design and implementation of custom IIoT solutions.
Participate in the deployment of IIoT applications at customer sites.
Help monitor and oversee customer projects, ensuring effective deployment and smooth operation.

What skills we need:
A Bachelor's or Master's degree in Electronic Engineering, Automation Engineering, Computer Science, Biomedical Engineering, or a related field (preferred but not mandatory).
A technical diploma in these fields, or relevant practical experience, will also be considered.
Proficiency in Python programming and basic knowledge of network API interfaces.
Fluency in English.
A proactive, self-starter attitude with strong communication skills and an innovative, out-of-the-box mindset.
Ability to work effectively within a multidisciplinary and dynamic team environment.

Bonus skills (a plus, but not mandatory):
Knowledge of Git, scripting (e.g., Bash, PowerShell), SQL, and experience with Docker.
Familiarity with technology integration, including mounting, cabling, wiring, hot-staging, and experience working with power and data signals.
Basic knowledge of industrial sensors and their applications.

Application process: the selection process includes an initial interview, followed by a skill assessment phase for shortlisted candidates.

Contractual offer:
Full-time Internship (3 or 6 months).
The internship includes an expense reimbursement to support your learning experience.

Location: Pisa, Italy. Presence at Zerynth's R&D offices in Pisa is required during the onboarding period and for specific planned activities.

About Zerynth
Zerynth supports manufacturing companies in streamlining production by increasing the value of interconnected industrial assets. Through the Zerynth Industrial IoT & AI Platform, it connects any industrial machinery in less than 3 hours, using a plug-and-play and non-invasive approach, enabling a complete 4.0 transformation with simplicity, speed and security.
Founded in 2015, Zerynth counts a team of over 40 experienced IoT professionals, strategic partnerships with system integrators and technology providers such as SAP, as well as over 150 customers across diverse industries: discrete manufacturing, food & beverage, logistics, utilities, and machinery manufacturing. Headquartered in Pisa, Italy, Zerynth is actively pursuing an ambitious international expansion project.
