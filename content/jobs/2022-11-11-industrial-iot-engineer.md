We are looking for you! 
If you are an engineer that wakes up in the morning with the sole purpose of transforming the jungle of industrial machines into usable data layers, look no further - Zerynth is your playground! We are seeking a motivated, talented engineer from whatever tribe you belong: automation, electrical, biomedical, etc. We promise plenty of electrical schemes, greasy screwdrivers, machine dungeons, and ancient PLCs to be tamed using the power of Zerynth’s IIoT. But be warned! At Zerynth everyone is also a salesman, because the customer is king. If working with customers scares you, don’t worry! There are other jobs from Zerynth here: https://it.zerynth.com/lavora-con-noi/



What you will do: 
Gain a solid understanding of customer workflow and use-case(s);
Gather clear technical requirements from the customer;
Design and implement IIoT solutions for customers;
Perform IIoT application development and installation at the customer’s premises;
Collaborate with the Sales Team to select the best product/solution for the customer’s needs;
Support and oversee the customer’s project once the solution is up and running;
Provide onsite and remote training;
Give customer knowledge and feedback to R&D for product improvement.



What skills we need:
Bachelor's or Master's degree in Electronic Engineering, Automation Engineering, Computer Science, Biomedical Engineering or similar education;
Coding skills, e.g. comfortable with Python, scripting/network API interfaces, SQL;
Experience with technology integration challenges: mounting, cabling, wiring, hot-staging, experience working with power & data;
General familiarity and experience with sensing applications;
Fluency in English;
Self-starter, good communication skills, able to think out-of-the-box, and a team player will round out your skill set;
Able to work in a multi-disciplinary, dynamic, team environment;
Remote assignments are possible during your tenure;


About Zerynth: 
Zerynth helps companies digitalize their production processes and develop connected industrial products through a plug-and-play IoT platform designed to enable the Industry 4.0 digital transformation for any industrial machine in a fast, flexible, and secure way.
Founded in 2015, Zerynth has grown steadily. Today it has 35+ team members with deep IoT expertise and industry knowledge with over 100 customers across many industries: from manufacturing to agriculture to energy to logistics. Zerynth is based in Pisa, Italy, but also is active in international projects, and foresees an expansion both in EU and non-EU countries during the next three years.
