Responsibilities

Our new team member will work directly with our Chief Technology Officer, and in collaboration with the rest of the engineering team, to design, enrich and build Cargoful tech suite.


Examples of activities you will perform:

- Develop new features and modules within our Web App, from back-end (Python) to UI/UX (React, Javascript)
- Develop new features and modules within our Mobile App (iOS and Android)
- Build our cloud big data pipeline
- Improve and maintain Cargoful's cloud architecture (running on AWS) by applying state-of-the-art DevOps methodologies and technologies (e.g., micro-services, containers)
- Develop and maintain automatic testing protocol for the Web App leveraging existing external frameworks (e.g., Playwright, Django docs, Jenkins)


Requirements

- Experience: 2-5 years developing Web Applications in Django or similar framework
- Education: Computer Science, Software Engineering (at least Bachelor’s degree)
- Language: At least B2 level (Intermediate) in English
- Set-up: mainly remote, 4/5 days per month in our office in Milan (Viale Monte Nero 32)
