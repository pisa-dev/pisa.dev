We are looking for you!
Zerynth is seeking a motivated, talented Full Stack Developer, interested in working on IoT focused applications and joining a fast-growing IoT company in our offices based in Pisa (Italy). We offer a challenging and creative job in an international environment that fosters the professional growth of its employees in a company culture valuing creative problem solving and open communication.



Primary Job Responsibilities:
Working in an agile team of talented software engineers
Development of mission critical large scale IoT applications 
Design and implementation of IoT application UX and UI
Implementation and management of IoT data streams and time series databases for Industrial IoT applications



Required Skills and Qualifications:
Experience with Go, Postgres, React;
Good knowledge of Linux and Docker;
Fluency in English;
Self-motivation, good communication, synthesis and cooperation;
Ability to work in multi-disciplinary, agile teams;
Experience with kafka and with Microservices will be a bonus point;
Experience in the IoT field or with IoT protocols will be a bonus point.



About Zerynth:
Zerynth helps companies digitalize their production processes and develop connected industrial products through a plug-and-play IoT platform designed to enable the Industry 4.0 digital transformation for any industrial machine in a fast, flexible, and secure way.
Founded in 2015, Zerynth has grown steadily. Today it has 35+ team members with deep IoT expertise and industry knowledge with over 100 customers across many industries: from manufacturing to agriculture to energy to logistics. Zerynth is based in Pisa, Italy, but also is active in international projects, and foresees an expansion both in EU and non-EU countries during the next three years.
