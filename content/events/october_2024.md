📢 Pisa.dev is back with a new Event! 🍂 🎃

⏰ Join us on October 29th at 18.00 in Aula G of the Polo Fibonacci for our meetup followed by dinner (location to be defined)

👀 The speaker will be Tommaso Radicioni, Data Scientist at AIKNOWYOU. Tommaso will guide us into the world of Large Language Models. In particular, he will explain what a RAG Application is and how to build one with Haystack!

🍕 As always, we'll wrap up the evening with pizza and beer at a local pizzeria.

Details:
Speaker: Tommaso Radicioni
Title: RAGTime: How to build a RAG application with Haystack
Abstract: Large Language Models often suffer from "hallucinations," generating outputs that are linguistically coherent but factually inaccurate. To prevent these hallucinations, we will explore together how Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) algorithms work by combining information retrieval with generative AI. We will also see how to build a simple RAG application using the Haystack framework!
