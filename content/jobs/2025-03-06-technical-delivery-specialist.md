We are looking for you!
Join Zerynth and grow in a dynamic company that is transforming the IoT industry! We are looking for a Technical Project & Delivery Specialist, a proactive and skilled professional who will play a key role in the implementation of Industrial IoT (IIoT) solutions for our customers.

What you will do:
Gain a comprehensive understanding of customer workflows and use cases.
Gather and document clear technical requirements from customers.
Provide technical support in the design and implementation of custom IIoT solutions.
Participate in the deployment and installation of IIoT applications at customer sites.
Monitor and oversee customer projects, ensuring effective deployment and optimal performance.
Collaborate with cross-functional teams to ensure seamless integration and continuous improvement.

What skills we need:
Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Electronic Engineering, Automation Engineering, Computer Science, Biomedical Engineering, or a related field.
A technical diploma in these fields, or relevant practical experience, will also be considered.
Proficiency in Python and basic knowledge of network API interfaces.
Fluency in English.
A problem-solving mindset with strong analytical and communication skills.
Ability to work independently and within multidisciplinary teams.

Bonus skills (a plus, but not mandatory):
Knowledge of Git, scripting (Bash, PowerShell), SQL, and Docker.
Familiarity with technology integration, including mounting, cabling, wiring, and experience with power and data signals.
Basic knowledge of industrial sensors and their applications.

Application process: Candidates will have a first interview. If successful, they will proceed to a skill assessment phase.

Contractual offer:
Full-time employment 
Presence at Zerynth's R&D offices in Pisa is required. Hybrid and flexible work options available.

About Zerynth
Zerynth supports manufacturing companies in streamlining production by increasing the value of interconnected industrial assets. Through the Zerynth Industrial IoT & AI Platform, it connects any industrial machinery in less than 3 hours, using a plug-and-play and non-invasive approach, enabling a complete 4.0 transformation with simplicity, speed and security.
Founded in 2015, Zerynth counts a team of over 40 experienced IoT professionals, strategic partnerships with system integrators and technology providers such as SAP, as well as over 150 customers across diverse industries: discrete manufacturing, food & beverage, logistics, utilities, and machinery manufacturing. Headquartered in Pisa, Italy, Zerynth is actively pursuing an ambitious international expansion project.
